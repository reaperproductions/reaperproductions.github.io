About Us

Reaper Productions is an independent horror production from Mikado, Michigan — run by Noah Yax, with his brother Ian as co-director.

We make horror films with practical effects, real locations, and stories that don’t let go. Our short The Abandoned (Part 1) played on the big screen at the Lake Theatre in Oscoda. Our next film, Night Shift — a 70+ minute morgue feature — is in production now.

We’re also the crew behind the Delirium Haunted Attraction.

Small crew. Big scares. Michigan-made.

The Abandoned (Part 1)

Horror Short · 17 Minutes · 2026

Dave can't shake his dreams about TJ and Max — two friends who explored the abandoned building before him. When Dave and his best friend Jackson follow in their footsteps, they find the place that got to TJ and Max isn't done yet. As its grip tightens on Dave, a trip with his best friend turns into something neither of them can walk away from. You don't control what happens here.

Night Shift
